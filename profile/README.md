## A creative workspace for video, images, and music

[VideoWeb AI](https://videoweb.ai/) brings AI video generation, image creation and editing, and music tools into a browser workspace for creators, marketers, ecommerce teams, and educators. Start from a written scene, a product photo, or a storyboard frame; choose a model and develop material for your next project without writing code.

Make a product reveal, a vertical social clip, a campaign image, or a lesson opener. Our GitHub resources help with the next question: **what should I ask the model to make, and what should I check in the result?**

- **A short scene from an idea** — [Text to Video](https://videoweb.ai/text-to-video/) — describe the subject, action, camera, and sound
- **Motion from a product photo or storyboard** — [Image to Video](https://videoweb.ai/image-to-video/) — supply your image and describe what moves
- **A thumbnail, poster, or revised product image** — [AI Image Generator](https://videoweb.ai/ai-image-generator/) — write a brief or direct an edit
- **Music for a creative project** — [AI Music Generator](https://videoweb.ai/ai-music/) — begin with the mood and musical direction

**Use it for:** Explore image concepts, photo edits, and style variations

**What to know before starting:** Page lists no signup; start with a prompt and an optional image.

## Choose a model for the work you want to do

Start with the material you have and the condition your output must meet. The options below come from the linked product pages; they are starting candidates, not a quality ranking. When several models fit, compare the same short brief in the available modes and check the displayed credit cost before generating.

### Video models

#### [Seedance 2.5](https://videoweb.ai/model/seedance-2-5/)

text/image video and reference-guided scene direction

**Your material and goal:** A written scene or product photo; you want to direct the reveal and camera

**What to confirm before generating:** Check that the selected mode accepts your reference type; describe one main action and a clear ending

#### [MiniMax H3](https://videoweb.ai/model/minimax-h3/)

the separate free workflow above is 5s / 480p

**Your material and goal:** Text or a first/last image pair; you want a short, free trial

**What to confirm before generating:** For longer or higher-resolution work, the model page describes up to 15s, 2K and native stereo audio; check the advanced form and credits

#### [Wan 3.0](https://videoweb.ai/model/wan-3-0/)

text/image video, 5–30s duration choices

**Your material and goal:** Opening image and a planned final composition; you need an end-frame option

**What to confirm before generating:** End-frame guidance is optional in supported image modes; confirm it is available in the mode you select

#### [Kling 3.0](https://videoweb.ai/model/kling-3-0/)

character action, audio and multi-shot storytelling

**Your material and goal:** A character scene or dialogue; you need multiple shots

**What to confirm before generating:** Check the selected mode's shot and audio controls; keep character appearance and speaker instructions explicit

#### [Veo 3.1](https://videoweb.ai/model/veo-3-1-video/)

cinematic text/image video with audio

**Your material and goal:** A scene brief or image; camera movement and ambient sound matter

**What to confirm before generating:** Confirm duration, audio settings and credit cost; H3 and Kling also describe audio, so sound alone does not decide the model

### Image models

#### [GPT Image 2.5](https://videoweb.ai/model/gpt-image-2-5/)

Flare for exploration, Sunburst for detailed editing

**Your material and goal:** A campaign image or one element to revise; you need to preserve the rest

**What to confirm before generating:** State the change and what must stay unchanged; check reference limits in the chosen form, especially the separate free entry

#### [Nano Banana Pro](https://videoweb.ai/nano-banana-pro-ai/)

image creation and reference combining

**Your material and goal:** Several visual references; you want to combine them into one composition

**What to confirm before generating:** Check upload limits and describe each image's role: subject, style or composition

#### [Seedream 5.0 Pro](https://videoweb.ai/model/seedream-5-0-pro/)

image generation and editing

**Your material and goal:** A product poster, information layout or storyboard

**What to confirm before generating:** Specify exact text and layout; review lettering and product details in the result

### Music

#### [Suno music workflows](https://videoweb.ai/ai-music/)

instrumental and vocal options; [pricing lists versions through 5.0](https://videoweb.ai/pricing/)

**Your material and goal:** A mood description or lyrics; you need a soundtrack

**What to confirm before generating:** Choose an available version and Instrumental or vocal mode; music is generated separately from the video

## Make your first five-second shot

Follow the [warm-room exercise in our free H3 guide](https://github.com/aivideoweb/best-free-minimax-h3/blob/main/docs/free-tool-prompts.md#user-content-videoweb). Its English/Chinese source is reproduced below so you can start here without searching the library. It is a text-only exercise, not a generated result.

1. Open [Free MiniMax H3](https://videoweb.ai/free-minimax-h3/). Leave both image slots empty.
2. Paste the prompt below and select landscape **16:9**. The free output is 5s / 480p.
3. Complete any verification, submit once and follow the request status. Preview the returned clip before downloading.
4. Check that the doorway stays straight, the camera moves smoothly and the chair and lamp are visible at the end. If the motion breaks, shorten the camera move for the next attempt.

```text
Create one five-second shot, 16:9.
From a still view outside an open wooden doorway, slowly push the camera into a quiet
reading room. A curtain moves gently at the window. End with the chair and lamp fully
visible. Keep the doorway straight and the room layout unchanged. Soft room tone; no
dialogue or text.
Keep the last second calm. No cuts or extra scene changes.
```

**Make it yours:** first replace “reading room” with your setting, such as a small shop. Replace “chair and lamp” with the objects you want visible at the end. Keep one continuous shot, the slow camera move and the calm final second. Change one instruction at a time and check the same details again.

This exercise has not been generation-tested. It creates a described scene; text alone does not preserve the appearance of a real room or product. For image guidance, supply both first and last frames in the free form. For a longer scene or different output settings, open the [H3 model page](https://videoweb.ai/model/minimax-h3/) and review the choices and credits.

**Need a soundtrack?** Open [AI Music](https://videoweb.ai/ai-music/), choose an available model and Instrumental mode, and describe, for example: “Warm acoustic guitar and soft percussion, relaxed mood, steady tempo, no vocals, for a calm product demonstration.” Check the displayed credit cost before generating, then review the music against your video. This music brief has not been generation-tested. Music creation is a separate workflow; adding it to your final edit is a later step.
